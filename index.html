The 2025 Fuso Super Great is Mitsubishi Fuso’s newest heavy-duty truck, fully redesigned in 2023 for better power, fuel efficiency, safety, and comfort.

  • Engine: 12.8 L 6R30 engine with higher torque and better fuel economy.
  • Efficiency: Improved aerodynamics, reduced emissions, and meets Japan’s 2025 fuel-efficiency standards.
  • Cab Design: New “Black Belt” front design with a Super High Roof option for more driver space and comfort.
  • Interior: More storage, modern materials, and ergonomic driver layout.
  • Safety: Equipped with latest ADAS features like Active Brake Assist 6, Side Guard Assist 2.0, and full camera systems for 360° awareness.

F-Series (FORWARD in Japan) medium-duty trucks support a wide range of applications including arterial transportation and inner-city delivery, as well as various construction tasks and operations. A variety of models are available, including a 4-bag air suspension model offering high-quality transportation and a short-cab model with increased load efficiency. The F-Series is especially well received in Asia and Australia. A full model change in 2023 significantly enhanced environmental performance, safety and economy.

  • Engine: 6-cylinder diesel (up to 6.7 L, 300 hp, 1,081 Nm torque).
  • Transmission: Automatic or AMT for smoother driving.
  • Capacity: Up to 19 tons GVW, suitable for urban or regional delivery.
  • Cab Design: Modern look with stronger chassis and more driver space.
  • Safety: Upgraded driver-assist features and reinforced structure for protection.

The Hino 700 Series is built for operators who demand proven reliability, advanced safety, and superior efficiency. The 700 Series offers more power, more capability, and more solutions to keep your business moving.
