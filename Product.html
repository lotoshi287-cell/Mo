Launched in 1959, the N-Series (ELF in Japan) is Japan's representative light-duty truck. Recognized for its outstanding combination of economy, safety, reliability and durability, it is the best-selling light-duty truck in numerous countries around the world.

  • Engine: 4-cylinder turbo diesel (4.8 L – 5.2 L), ~135–150 PS power.
  • Capacity: GVW between 7.5 t and 8.5 t, depending on model.
  • Transmission: Manual or automatic options.
  • Fuel System: Common-rail injection + intercooler for better economy and low emissions.
  • Body Types: Flatbed, cargo box, tipper, and microbus conversions.
